# ResearchHub Submission

## Product

Decentralized research grant & collaboration lifecycle on Stellar/Soroban.

## Comparison note

Peers like [ImpactChain](https://github.com/Nikkunj-145/ImpactChain/) excel at CSR/grant transparency. ResearchHub targets the **research** lifecycle with six contracts (including peer review + publications), factory→treasury allocation, protocol fees, and an empty-by-default console.
